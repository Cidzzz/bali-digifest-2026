# DESIGN SYSTEM CONTRACT: KriyaLoka AI

## 0. Research Log & Overview
- Domain: Balinese Cultural Heritage Handicraft Exporter & AI Multimodal Copilot (Bali DigiFest 2026).
- Target Archetype: Balinese artisanal elegance blended with modern international export dashboard (anti-generic AI slop, anti-chatbot bubble).
- Heritage Palette:
  - Terracotta Majapahit: `#C85A32` (Primary brand accent, energetic artisan warmth)
  - Dark Terracotta: `#B45309` (Active borders, focused typography)
  - Prada Gold: `#D97706` / `#F59E0B` (Traditional Balinese gilt gold, premium authenticity)
  - Ubud Green: `#15803D` / Emerald `#059669` (Material sustainability, export clearance, fair price value)
  - Lontar Cream: `#FAF8F5` (Warm parchment background, avoiding clinical cold white)
  - Surface Card: `#FFFFFF` with warm stone-200 subtle borders and soft shadows.

## 1. Typography & Hierarchy
- Font Family: Geist Sans / Inter / -apple-system with Serif touches for certificates.
- Heading Hero: `text-3xl sm:text-4xl font-extrabold tracking-tight text-stone-900`.
- Card Titles: `text-base font-bold text-stone-900 flex items-center gap-2`.
- Badges: `text-[10px] tracking-wider uppercase font-bold px-2.5 py-0.5 rounded-full`.

## 2. Interactive Primitives
- Judge Preset Cards: 3 sample cards placed prominently above the results with clear active ring and background gradient.
- Drag & Drop Zone: File input clickable across whole area with explicit input ref, camera modal with stream lifecycle management.
- Multilingual Tabs: Distinct tab buttons for `en` (🇬🇧), `jp` (🇯🇵), `zh` (🇨🇳), and `id` (🇮🇩) with instant state feedback and clipboard copying.
- Print Certificate: Clean isolated print media styling with QR code mockup, verified stamp, and attribution.
